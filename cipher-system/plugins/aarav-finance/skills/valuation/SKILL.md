# Valuation

State the valuation date, share count basis, net debt/cash, currency, units,
and source period. Prefer reported values over estimates and label consensus
assumptions.

Use multiple lenses where appropriate:

- DCF with explicit revenue, margin, reinvestment, WACC, terminal growth, and
  sensitivity tables.
- Trading comps with metric definitions, fiscal period alignment, and outlier
  treatment.
- SOTP when segments have materially different economics.
- Reverse DCF or reverse multiple analysis to show what the current price
  requires.

Do not collapse uncertainty into one target price. Report ranges, drivers,
break-even assumptions, and the evidence that could change them.
