# Equity research

Use Equibles or SEC primary documents for structured financial series and
filings. Use Cipher for local market and options context.

Cover business model, segment economics, secular drivers, competitive position,
growth quality, margins, cash flow, balance sheet, dilution, capital
allocation, catalysts, risks, and what would invalidate the thesis.

Valuation should include at least a base case and sensitivities. Cite every
material numeric claim with source and period. If estimates are unavailable,
label the gap rather than substituting a remembered number.
