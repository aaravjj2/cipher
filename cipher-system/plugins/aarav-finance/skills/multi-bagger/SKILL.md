# Multi-bagger discovery

This is a research funnel, not a prediction engine.

1. Define the secular buildout and time horizon.
2. Map the value chain and identify bottlenecks or picks-and-shovels suppliers.
3. Verify economic exposure using filings, customers, contracts, backlog,
   capacity, pricing, and segment disclosures.
4. Review growth quality: revenue, gross margin, operating leverage, cash
   conversion, working capital, capex, dilution, and balance sheet.
5. Check moat, competition, concentration, cyclicality, and execution risk.
6. Calculate the market-cap and per-share math for 2x, 3x, and 5x outcomes.
7. Reverse-underwrite today's price: what growth, margin, duration, and dilution
   are already implied?
8. Separate price dislocation from thesis deterioration.
9. Rank candidates by evidence quality and asymmetric upside/downside, not by
   cheapness alone.

Every candidate must include disconfirming evidence, valuation sensitivities,
and the next observable milestone. Never invent a contract, customer, TAM, or
forecast.
