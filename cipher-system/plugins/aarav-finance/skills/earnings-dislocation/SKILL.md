# Earnings dislocation

Build a point-in-time comparison.

## Before earnings

Record consensus and management guidance, implied growth and margins,
valuation, expected move, IV/skew, positioning, and the key thesis variables.

## Actual release

Compare revenue, margins, bookings/backlog, guidance, capex, customers,
capacity, cash flow, and management commentary against the pre-event record.

## After earnings

Measure price and IV reaction, analyst revisions, and any Cipher/FlashAlpha
GEX/VEX or flow change. Mark each thesis variable stronger, unchanged,
weakened, or broken.

The conclusion must explicitly classify the event as: price down/opportunity
down; price down/opportunity unchanged; or price down/opportunity improved.
Use the same framework for upside gaps. Distinguish facts from interpretation
and preserve timestamps so later research cannot leak future information.
