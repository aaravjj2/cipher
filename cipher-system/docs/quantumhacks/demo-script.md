# QuantumHacks demo script — target 3:30

## 0:00–0:25 — problem and promise

“A normal options trader has to reconcile a screener, chart, option chain, news,
exposure data, spreadsheets, and AI chat. The dangerous failure is not a missing
feature—it is false confidence when those tools use stale or incomplete data.
Cipher is an all-in-one options research copilot designed around auditability.”

Show the signed-in **Morning Brief**. Point to session state, freshness, and the
paper-only badge.

## 0:25–0:55 — find an opportunity

Open **Setup Scanner**. Show the funnel from universe to data-qualified,
liquidity-qualified, setup-qualified, and ranked. Open one candidate and point to
its evidence ID, event time, coverage, invalidation, and rejection reasons.

Say: “Cipher does not hide the denominator. A missing quote is not a zero and a
rejected candidate remains part of the audit.”

## 0:55–1:30 — validate the same evidence

Open **Night Vision** from the candidate. Show the price-first chart, session
levels, exposure bands, and frozen replay banner.

Say: “This is the same evidence snapshot—not a chart recomputed after I clicked.
GEX is explicitly a public-open-interest heuristic, never claimed as verified
dealer positioning.”

## 1:30–2:00 — structure the idea

Open **Options Terminal**. Show expiration/strike liquidity, bid/ask spread,
OI date, volume, Greeks/IV availability, and a defined-risk structure.

Say: “Cipher rejects wide, stale, or unaffordable contracts. If a single long
option exceeds its paper allocation, it can seek a same-expiry vertical while
preserving the maximum debit.”

## 2:00–2:50 — autonomous but bounded

Open **Paper Portfolios**. Show:

- premarket plan and decision-trace status;
- realized, midpoint-marked, and liquidation equity;
- daily two-loss lock and 11:30 entry cutoff;
- one complete position/fill record;
- skipped-signal outcomes;
- normalized cohorts labelled TINY and not rankable.

Say: “The autopilot discovers before the open, waits for a fresh closed-bar
confirmation, and trades only this simulator. It cannot reach a broker order.
Even when a strategy looks good, Cipher refuses to rank it before its prospective
sample gate.”

## 2:50–3:20 — AI and impact

Open **Research Desk** or the current memo. Show cited evidence, contradictions,
missing inputs, and human review state.

Say: “FinBERT contributes advisory news context; it cannot authorize an entry.
The long-term value is giving individual traders institutional research habits:
source awareness, invalidations, liquidity checks, and honest uncertainty.”

## 3:20–3:30 — close

“Cipher is not an AI stock picker. It is an auditable decision system for the
person who still owns the decision.”
